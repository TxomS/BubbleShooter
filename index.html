How to play

  • Aim with the mouse or by dragging a finger. The dotted line shows your path, bounces included.
  • Shoot with a click, or lift your finger. Drag back below the launcher to cancel. Keyboard: arrows and space.
  • Match 3 or more of the same planet to pop them in a supernova.
  • Cut loose anything hanging from popped planets. It falls, and it's worth bonus points.
  • Watch DESCENT. Each shot that doesn't pop lights one. When they're all lit, the ceiling drops a row.
  • Stay above the event horizon. If planets cross the dashed red line, the signal is lost.
  • Pick a mode. Easy to Impossible each have five levels. Endless never stops: clear a wave and the next one glides in.
